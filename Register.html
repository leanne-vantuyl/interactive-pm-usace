A message from your proponent

Welcome to Interactive PM in USACE.

Project management in USACE is both a science and an art. The science is learnable -- the PDBP lifecycle, PROC references, earned value, risk registers, change control. We can teach you the thresholds, the reporting requirements, the decision gates. That part has a right answer.

The art is harder. It is the judgment call at 4:45 on a Friday. It is knowing when to push back and when to hold. It is reading a room, managing up, and keeping a PDT moving when the schedule slips and the customer is watching. That part cannot be taught from a slide -- it has to be practiced.

This course gives you thirteen decisions. Each one is grounded in USACE doctrine. Each one is also a moment where the science alone will not be enough. Work through them honestly -- not to find the "right" answer, but to find your answer, and then to understand why it matters.

Leanne Van Tuyl, PMP
PPM Community of Practice · HQ USACE
