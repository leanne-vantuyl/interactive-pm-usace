For Facilitators & Self-Paced Learners

Each case file runs 20–30 minutes and is self-contained — training, reflection, branching scenario, and a 5-question Knowledge Check (60% to pass, with shuffled answer order). The full course is roughly 5.5–7 hours and maps to the Initiation, Planning, Execution, and Closeout phases of the PDBP lifecycle.

Recommended use: complete 1–2 case files per week as a self-paced alternative to classroom PROSPECT 355 sessions, or use individual case files as discussion-starters in PDT or CCB training sessions. The storyline is continuous — case files reference earlier ones — but each stands on its own if used out of sequence.
