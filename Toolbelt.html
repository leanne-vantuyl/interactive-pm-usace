The PM's Toolbelt

These are the real systems USACE PMs use day to day. Rather than a slide of acronyms, each tool here is linked to the moment in this course where a PM is actually shown using it — and why.

Used by every PM
PROMIS / Primavera P6

The PDT's scheduling engine — builds and maintains the resource-loaded baseline, calculates Schedule Variance and SPI automatically (the float/critical-path math behind PROC2030 and PROC3000).

CF04Built to Slip →CF07The Phantom Slip →
CEFMS / CEFMS II

Corps of Engineers Financial Management System — the system of record for project cost, obligations, and expenditures.

CF05The Number Doesn't Fit →
CCIR #8

Command Channel Information Requirement #8 — a recurring project status reporting requirement that rolls schedule and cost data up to leadership.

CF07The Phantom Slip →
RMS Reports

Resident Management System reports — construction-phase tracking of contractor progress, submittals, and quality data.

Not yet directly featured in a case file.

Civil Works
CWIFD

Civil Works Integrated Funding Database — where a PM tracks funding by Authorization/Appropriation/Apportionment and identifies exactly where a funding gap sits in that chain.

CF05The Number Doesn't Fit →
Enterprise Risk Register

The Civil Works-wide risk tracking system — where informal risk knowledge ("this vendor usually runs late") becomes a formal entry with an owner, trigger, and response plan. Covers both threats (Avoid/Transfer/Mitigate/Accept) and opportunities (Exploit/Share/Enhance/Accept).

CF08The Risk Nobody Tracked →CF11Two Faces of Risk →
Military Programs
CMI

Construction Management Information system — Military Programs construction tracking and reporting.

Not yet directly featured in a case file.

PAX

Programming and execution tracking tool used in Military Programs for project status.

Not yet directly featured in a case file.

ProjNet / DrChecks

Design review and comment-resolution platform — used to coordinate design submittals and track reviewer comments to closure.

Not yet directly featured in a case file.

Planning Reference
PMP Builder (Walla Walla District)

A template/reference tool for assembling the 14-component Project Management Plan — a good starting point for any new PDT building out their PMP.
